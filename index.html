About this cipher

This application uses a custom key-based, position-dependent cipher. Uppercase letters, lowercase letters, and digits are transformed using a deterministic key stream combined with the character's position.

The same plaintext character can therefore produce different ciphertext characters at different positions.

Spaces, punctuation, symbols, emoji, and other unsupported characters remain unchanged and do not advance the cipher position.

Educational use only

This is an educational custom cipher and is not suitable for protecting sensitive information.

Privacy

All encryption and decryption happens locally in your browser. Your text and encryption key are not uploaded to a server or sent to an API.
